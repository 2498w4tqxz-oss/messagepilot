# Physical phone acceptance — 2026-10-07

Testing used an iPhone 14 Pro running iOS 27.0 and exactly one authorized self-conversation. The Mac retained SIP protection. All test messages went to that self-conversation; no other conversations, Contacts, personal photo library or Find My people were opened. Effects testing stopped when the user requested focus on other features.

Two routes were exercised: the MessagePilot gateway and chat-restricted Mac worker, and the separately installed phone-control skill operating native iPhone Messages. **Phone-control success establishes native capability, not an implemented or accepted MessagePilot phone adapter.** A self-conversation also does not establish behavior between independent Apple identities.

## Remaining acceptance and concrete blockers

- **Custom interactive carousel and primary app port:** the physical build failed provisioning. Xcode on the device host reported “No Accounts”; the cached profile did not support the required App Groups and Push Notifications entitlements. MessagePilot was not installed on this phone. Required entitlements were not removed to bypass the failure. The simulator's blank inline carousel remains unresolved even though its extension pages and callback worked.
- **Polls:** prior Mac submission attempts failed. No Polls entry was available in the inspected phone app menu for this self-conversation. Sending, voting and adding choices remain unproven.
- **Send Later:** prior Mac scheduling and pending-entry deletion passed. No Send Later entry was available in the inspected phone app menu. Date editing, Send Now and actual timed delivery remain unproven.
- **Own generated stickers:** the earlier simulator rendering result remains; physical delivery through the MessagePilot extension is blocked by provisioning. Native stock-sticker success does not close this gap.
- **Other Apple and third-party features:** Genmoji, Image Playground, standalone GIPHY, real camera/audio capture, shared app editing and arbitrary installed apps remain unverified. No Apple Cash transaction was attempted.
- **Widgets, Dynamic Island, ARKit, RoomPlan, biometrics and APNs:** previous simulator results retain their limits. Physical MessagePilot host acceptance requires successful provisioning/install and the applicable hardware, permissions or service configuration. Passkeys additionally need a configured relying party, associated domains and a real system authentication ceremony.
- **Identity and lifecycle:** independent Apple identities, multi-account isolation, guest installation/activation, virtual-computer takeover and reboot recovery remain unverified. Restricted incoming-message streaming and history pagination remain unimplemented.
